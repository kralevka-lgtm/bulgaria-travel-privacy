🇧🇬 Bulgaria Travel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Bulgaria Travel is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Location Data

We request access to your device's location ONLY to show your position on the interactive map. Location data is NOT stored or shared with third parties.

Usage Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience. This data cannot identify you personally.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect personal information
  • We do NOT require registration or login
  • We do NOT store location history
  • We do NOT sell data to third parties
  • We do NOT display advertisements

4. Third Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — database and analytics
    Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Open-Meteo — weather data (no personal data collected)
    Privacy Policy: open-meteo.com/privacy
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles (no personal data collected)
    Privacy Policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page.
